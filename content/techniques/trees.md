## Coach
Plant a trunk, then branches that get thinner, and only then the leaves.

## History
A tree drawn as a lollipop has no weight. The old method is structure, then masses, then value, then a person for scale.

## World
Sketchbook studies, street trees, and any canopy that has to sit on a trunk.
